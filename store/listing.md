## Full description (max 4000)

Never forget to send that message again.

ChatLater lets you write messages ahead of time and reminds you exactly when to send them. When it's time, tap the notification and WhatsApp opens with the chat and your message already typed. Just press send.

PERFECT FOR
• Birthday and anniversary wishes at midnight
• Good-morning messages to family
• Meeting, payment and rent reminders for clients and tenants
• Festival greetings
• Anything you want to remember to say later

FEATURES
• Pick a contact or type any number, with the country code added automatically
• Message any chat or group: WhatsApp opens its chat list with your message ready
• Repeat daily, weekly, monthly or yearly
• Quick times: in 1 hour, tonight, midnight, tomorrow morning, next Monday
• Templates with {name}, filled in with each person's first name
• "Send now" button right on the notification
• Snooze for 15 minutes, an hour or until tonight
• A gentle follow-up nudge if you forget to send
• Missed messages wait under "Ready to send" until you send or skip them
• History of everything you've sent, with "schedule again" in one tap
• Search, light and dark themes
• Works with WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-up
• Your messages and contacts never leave your phone
• No ads, no tracking

WHY ONE TAP?
WhatsApp doesn't allow other apps to send from your personal account. Apps that tap "send" for you break WhatsApp's terms and can get your number banned. ChatLater keeps your account safe: it does all the remembering and typing, and you press send.

ChatLater is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta. WhatsApp is a trademark of WhatsApp LLC.
